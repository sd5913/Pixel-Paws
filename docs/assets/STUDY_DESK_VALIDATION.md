roughness 0.78–0.82, metalness 0, material count and texture count. The preview
loads the unchanged mug at `[0.58,0.75,0.27]`, leaving the center and front of the
1.80 × 0.90 m tabletop clear for the future cat.

and 75° elevation in 390×844 and 360×800 portrait viewports. All six views loaded

and the five collider overlays aligned with the tabletop and four legs. A cat does
not exist yet, so front-paw animation visibility remains a future character check.
