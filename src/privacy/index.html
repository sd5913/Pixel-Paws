Privacy Policy

Pixel Paws Desk Cleaner (also known as Pixel Paws) is a small game about a cat clearing a desk. This policy explains how the iOS game handles information.

Effective date: 23 November 2026Last updated: 23 November 2026

Introduction

This policy applies to the Pixel Paws Desk Cleaner iOS app with bundle ID org.sd5913.pixelpaws. Core gameplay does not require an internet connection.

Information We Collect

The game does not require an account or login. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other personal information through the game. It does not send gameplay information to our servers.

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and message outside the game so we can respond.

Local Storage of Game Data

Best scores and achievement progress are stored locally on your device. Game settings and appearance choices are handled on your device; some choices apply only to the current session. Locally stored gameplay data is not transmitted to our servers.

Analytics, Advertising and Third-Party Services

The game does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services, and it does not integrate third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. This policy page has no tracking scripts, cookies, or externally loaded fonts. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process ordinary requests to serve it under GitHub's own privacy practices.

TestFlight and Beta Testing

If you install a beta through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may independently process installation details, device information, crash diagnostics, and feedback you choose to submit. Apple may make some beta feedback and diagnostics available to the developer. Apple's handling of this information is separate from the game's data practices described above.

Data Security

Game records stay on your device and are not uploaded to a game server. Their protection depends in part on your device's security settings. You can reset achievement progress in the game's Settings screen; removing the app or its data may also remove local records.

Children's Privacy

The game does not ask children to provide personal information, and it does not use advertising or tracking services. If you have a concern about a child's privacy, please contact us using the address below.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the game's privacy practices change, we will update this page and its last updated date. Please check this page for the current policy.

Contact Us

For questions about this policy, email shuniii@outlook.com.
