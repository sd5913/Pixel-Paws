THE PLAN (SORT OF)

It's a very simple job.

For a cat, the execution is everything.

01 ⌖

Find your target

Guide your cat around the desktop and pick a prop that looks especially knockable.

02 ✳

Give it a swat

Get close, then tap the swat button (or press K) to send it tumbling.

03 ✦

Chase a new high

Topple props, trigger combos, and see how much desk you can clear before time's up.

⌨ Use the on-screen joystick, WASD, or arrow keys to move. Drag farther to dash.
